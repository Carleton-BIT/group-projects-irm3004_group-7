Health and Counselling Services

Description

Health and Counselling Services (HCS) is located in the Carleton Training and Technology Centre (CTTC) in Suite 2600. It is a multidisciplinary healthcare facility that provides medical services to Carleton University students, faculty and staff. Our services include access to same-day appointments, primary care appointments, nursing visits and lab testing.

Contact

Health and Counselling Services (HCS) is open for in person health services. You can now book a medical appointment through the CHR Connect App or through the CHR Connect Website.

If you do not see any appointments available, or for other inquiries, please call our office at: 613-520-6674 ext 0, between the hours of Monday-Friday, 8:30am-4:30pm.

For general inquiries, please email: hcs@carleton.ca.

Fax Number: 613-520-3851

Website: https://wellness.carleton.ca/health/health-clinic-services/

Location

Address: 2600 Carleton Training and Technology Centre, Carleton University
